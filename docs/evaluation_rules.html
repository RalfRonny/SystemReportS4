How each figure is collected

Reference for the S/4HANA Data & Org Questionnaire report on SAP ECC. For every figure: what is counted, from which table and field, and what is deliberately left out. Only SAP standard tables are read; the report outputs counts, never amounts or names of customers, vendors or users.

Basics

TermMeaning
Analysis periodFrom the 1st of a month to the last day of a month, ending before the current month. Default when the dates are empty: the last 12 complete months. The period of a run is shown under INFO in view 4.
MonthsNumber of calendar months in the period. Divisor for column C.
Column B – current numberStock on the run date. Master data: current count. Documents: the number open today, regardless of when they were created.
Column C – average per monthRecords created in the period ÷ months, rounded commercially.
Total in periodRecords created in the period (absolute), so that small averages that round to 0 stay visible.
Created in periodCreation or entry date of the record within the period, e.g. ERDAT, CPUDT (entry date), AEDAT for EKKO.
Active company codeCompany code (T001) with at least one FI document entered in the period (BKPF-CPUDT). Charts of accounts, banks, plants and countries are taken from the active company codes only, so template and sample company codes drop out.
ClientEverything is counted in the client of the run. Exceptions (system-wide): RFC destinations, logical systems, software components, charts of accounts texts and other client-independent tables.

Cell values

N/A

Not applicable by design of the questionnaire, or not derivable from SAP standard tables (e.g. P-card transactions, lock boxes, employees).

not collected

The block was switched off in the selection (scope checkboxes). Never shown as 0.

error

The collection step failed. The step and the error text are listed under INFO in view 4.

Frequent questions

Why are there so many products (row 10)?
Row 10 counts every material type. Technical, office, non-valuated and service materials are included. Use the split per material type in view 4 (D03) to separate sellable products.
Why does row 13 seem low?
Only sales document category C (order) counts. Contracts, returns and credit/debit memo requests are excluded (see D07). Deliveries (D08) are usually higher because of partial deliveries and stock transport orders. Column B only counts orders that are still open today.
Why is a sales organization not marked as used?
"Used" needs at least one sales order (category C) created in the period with that sales organization in VBAK. An organization that only exists in Customizing (no VBAK entries) is correctly not used.
Why is row 28 much lower than the number of users?
Only dialog users count that are valid today, not locked by an administrator and logged on since the period start. Reference, system, service and communication users are excluded (see D25, D26). Only the client of the run is read.
Why is row 4 only 1 although there are country charts?
Row 4 counts operational charts of accounts (T001-KTOPL). The cell shows them as "1 (4 local, 1 group)": country and group charts are in brackets, their keys in the remark and in D28/D29.
What does "not collected" mean?
The block was switched off in the selection. Run again with all scope checkboxes set.
A figure shows "error". What now?
View 4 INFO lists the failed step and its error text. Send that line along with the system ID.
